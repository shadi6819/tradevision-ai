MONEY MANAGEMENT MODULE

Add an advanced Money Management section before the screenshot upload.

The user must be able to enter:

* Current Account Balance
* Account Currency (USD, BDT, EUR, etc.)
* Maximum Risk Per Trade (%) (Optional)
* Experience Level
    * Beginner
    * Intermediate
    * Advanced

After analyzing the uploaded chart, the AI must also generate a professional Money Management Report.

Position Size Recommendation

Based on the user’s balance and the confidence level of the analysis, recommend:

* Suggested Trade Amount
* Maximum Safe Trade Amount
* Conservative Trade Amount
* Aggressive Trade Amount

The AI must explain why those amounts are suggested.

Example:

Current Balance:
$100

Recommended Trade:
$2

Maximum Safe Trade:
$3

Aggressive Option:
$5

Reason:
The detected setup has medium confidence and moderate risk, so risking only a small percentage of the account is preferable.

⸻

Risk Management

Display:

* Recommended Risk %
* Maximum Daily Risk
* Maximum Consecutive Losses Before Stopping
* Recommended Daily Trade Limit
* Whether the setup is worth taking or skipping

⸻

Martingale Evaluation

The AI must analyze whether using Martingale would be appropriate for this specific setup.

Display one of:

* ✅ Martingale Not Recommended
* ⚠️ Martingale Use With Extreme Caution
* ⚪ Martingale Optional (High-Risk)
* ❌ Avoid Martingale Completely

Then explain why.

If Martingale is considered, suggest a maximum number of recovery steps.

Example:

Recommended Recovery Steps:
0

or

Maximum Recovery Steps:
1

or

Maximum Recovery Steps:
2

Never recommend unlimited Martingale.

The explanation must consider:

* Confidence level
* Market conditions
* Volatility
* Chart quality
* Probability of consecutive losses

⸻

Session Advice

Recommend:

* Whether to trade now or wait
* Whether to reduce position size
* Whether to skip the trade entirely
* Whether market conditions are too risky

⸻

Capital Protection

If the setup has low confidence or elevated risk, the AI should prioritize protecting capital instead of encouraging a trade.

Example:

Recommended Action:
NO TRADE

Reason:
Current market structure lacks sufficient confirmation. Preserving capital is the better decision.

⸻

Final Trading Plan

Generate a concise trading plan including:

* Trade Decision (BUY / SELL / UP / DOWN / NO TRADE)
* Confidence Score
* Suggested Trade Amount
* Suggested Expiry (Binary) or Entry/SL/TP (Forex)
* Risk Level
* Martingale Recommendation
* Maximum Recovery Steps
* Daily Risk Reminder
* Brief explanation of the overall strategy

Important Behavior Rules

* Never encourage increasing trade size after a loss.
* Never imply that losses can be recovered with certainty.
* Never recommend unlimited or guaranteed-profit Martingale strategies.
* Always prioritize disciplined risk management and capital preservation over taking more trades.
* Base all money management suggestions on the user’s provided balance, selected market type, and the confidence of the chart analysis.
